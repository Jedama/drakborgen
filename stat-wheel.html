Stat wheel prototype

Sixteen stats in four categories. Each notch raises the stat it sits on and spreads across its neighbours according to its own curve. Wide notches use bells and define a region; narrow notches use convex spikes and define a single stat. Every notch peaks at the same height, so width alone decides total influence and the primary always sets the shape.
